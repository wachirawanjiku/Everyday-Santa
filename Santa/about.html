About Us

Everyday Santa

Our Vision

To be Kenya’s leading online gifting platform, transforming how people express love and appreciation through thoughtfully curated gifts. We aim to make every gifting experience effortless, joyful, and unforgettable, ensuring that every present tells a unique story.

Our Approach

At EVERYDAY SANTA, we make gifting thoughtful, seamless, and personal.Every hamper is carefully curated to reflect the recipient’s personality, ensuring a meaningful touch. We prioritize personalization,quality, and convenience, offering high-end products and an effortless shopping experience to create unforgettable moments.

Our Process

Gifting with us is simple. Browse our collection, personalize your gift with custom options, schedule delivery at your convenience, and let your loved ones unwrap the joy of a beautifully curated present. EVERYDAY SANTA makes every gift special.
